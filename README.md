# 🍽️ MealWise

### Smart Canteen Food Management & Prediction System

MealWise is a smart web-based canteen management system designed to reduce food waste and improve canteen profitability. The system analyzes historical food sales and consumption data to predict future food demand, estimate food waste, and calculate overall profit.

## 🚀 Features

- 📊 Food demand prediction based on historical data
- ♻️ Food waste prediction
- 💰 Profit and revenue calculation
- 📈 Historical sales and consumption analysis
- 🍱 Smart food quantity recommendations
- 📋 Canteen management dashboard
- 📊 Data visualization and analytics

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Canteens often prepare food based on assumptions or previous experience, which can result in overproduction, unnecessary food waste, and financial losses.

MealWise aims to solve this problem by using historical data to help canteen managers make data-driven decisions about how much food should be prepared.

## 💡 How MealWise Works

1. Collect historical food sales and consumption data.
2. Analyze previous demand and waste patterns.
3. Predict expected demand for upcoming days.
4. Estimate possible food waste.
5. Calculate revenue, expenses, waste cost, and profit.
6. Provide recommendations for better food preparation.

## 🧠 Prediction System

The system uses historical data such as:

- Previous sales
- Food quantity prepared
- Quantity sold
- Quantity wasted
- Day of the week
- Food item
- Price
- Cost of preparation

These factors are analyzed to predict future demand and potential waste.

## 💰 Profit Calculation

MealWise calculates:

**Revenue = Quantity Sold × Selling Price**

**Total Cost = Food Preparation Cost + Waste Cost**

**Profit = Revenue − Total Cost**

This helps identify which food items generate higher profits and which items contribute to unnecessary losses.
